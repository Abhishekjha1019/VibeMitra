## 2. What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), display name, date of birth, city.
- **Profile and activity:** profile details you add, discovery preferences, and (when built) matches and messages.
- **Technical data:** access tokens (kept on your device in secure storage), request logs and error reports.

We do not collect precise location in the current version.

## 3. Why we use it
- To create and run your account, and to show you matches in your chosen city.
- To confirm you are 18 or older, which is required before you can use matching features.
- To keep the service secure and to investigate abuse reports.
